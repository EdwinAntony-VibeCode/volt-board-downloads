Frequently asked questions.

What is Volt Board?

Volt Board is an Android digital whiteboard application for drawing, writing and organizing ideas collaboratively.

How do I collaborate with others?

You can host a new room which generates a 6-digit code. Share this code with students or teammates so they can join live.
